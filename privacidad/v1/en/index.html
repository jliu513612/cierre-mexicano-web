Privacy notice

Version 1, in effect from [POR COMPLETAR: fecha de publicación] to [POR COMPLETAR: fecha de publicación]. It is no longer in effect: see the current version.

What data the taxcerteza.mx website collects, what it is used for, who it is shared with and how to exercise your rights.

1. Who is responsible

The party responsible for your personal data is [POR COMPLETAR: razón social], with its address at [POR COMPLETAR: domicilio]. For any matter related to this notice, write to [POR COMPLETAR: correo para derechos ARCO].

This notice covers the taxcerteza.mx website: its pages, its free tools, the blog and the help center. It does not cover the Tax Certeza desktop program.

2. What data the site collects today

The site counts downloads, tool uses and clicks to learn what is useful. These records do not include your name, your IP address, a fingerprint calculated from it, or your browser identifier. This is what is stored in each case, and nothing else:

When you download the program

  • the date and time;
  • whether it happened on the published site or on a test preview;
  • the version and the file you downloaded;
  • the page of this site where you clicked the button;
  • the country, as a two-letter code (for example, MX). Our hosting provider infers it from the connection at that moment; we store only the country code, not the IP address;
  • the type of request;
  • whether the request looks like it comes from an automated program (a search engine, for example). This is worked out at that moment from the browser identifier, which is not stored;
  • whether the browser belongs to our own team, so our tests are not counted.

When you use a tool

  • which tool you used and, for some tools, which action (for example, downloading a range of exchange rates);
  • how many items it processed (for example, how many XML files), without their content;
  • the date and time, whether it was the published site or a test preview, the country, whether it looks like an automated program and whether it is our own team, as with downloads.

What you type or load into a tool (an RFC, an amount, your XML files) is processed in your browser and is not sent to the site.

When you click a contact or download link

  • where the link goes (WhatsApp, the demo, the download or the video);
  • the page of this site where you clicked it;
  • the date and time, whether it was the published site or a test preview, the country, whether it looks like an automated program and whether it is our own team.

When you answer «Was this result useful?»

  • the tool, the type of result you saw and your vote;
  • if you vote no, the reason you write. Anything shaped like an RFC is removed before it is sent. Please do not write personal data there;
  • the date.

No email address or any other contact detail is requested.

When you write to us

If you write to us by WhatsApp or email, we receive your number or email address, the name your account shows and what you write. We use them to answer you.

The site does not ask for sensitive personal data.

3. What is stored in your browser

The site does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics cookies. There is a single cookie, and it exists only in our own team's browsers so their downloads are not counted. The help center may store your light or dark theme preference in your browser; that preference does not leave your device.

4. Planned change: returning visits (not active yet)

We want to know, in aggregate, how many people come back to the site. For that, the site may later store in your browser's storage a random identifier that contains none of your data. No IP address, no browser identifier and no third-party services. It will only be used to count, per month, new visitors, returning visitors and visits per visitor. If your browser has the Do Not Track signal or Global Privacy Control turned on, the identifier will not be created. You will be able to delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

This is not active today. Before turning it on we will publish a new version of this notice.

5. What we use the data for

  • To know how many people download the program and use the tools, and which pages lead to the download, as aggregate figures.
  • To improve the tools with the opinions we receive.
  • To answer your messages.

Secondary purpose: if you write to us (and, once the site has registration, if you register), we may send you messages about Tax Certeza products and updates. You can unsubscribe at any time through the same channel the messages reach you on, or by writing to [POR COMPLETAR: correo para derechos ARCO]. Unsubscribing does not affect the other purposes.

We do not sell your data or transfer it to third parties.

6. Who it is shared with

We do not transfer your data to third parties. To run the site we use providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Netlify: hosts the site, runs the counter and receives the opinions. Like any server, it receives the technical data of the connection in order to deliver the pages to you.
  • GitHub: delivers the program file when you download it. Your browser connects directly to GitHub to fetch it.

The home page shows a YouTube video. When it loads, your browser connects to YouTube (Google), which receives the connection data under its own privacy policy. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. The links to social networks only take you there if you click them.

7. How to limit the use of your data

  • The site's records do not identify you, so you do not need to configure anything to use it.
  • You can leave the reason empty in «Was this result useful?», or not answer at all.
  • Once returning-visit counting is active, you will be able to avoid it with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • You can unsubscribe from messages about products and updates through the same channel they reach you on, or at the email in section 8.
  • You can ask us to stop using your contact details (section 8).

8. Your ARCO rights

You have the right to access your personal data, rectify it, cancel it or object to its use. To exercise these rights, write to [POR COMPLETAR: correo para derechos ARCO] with:

  • your name and a way to reply to you;
  • a document that proves your identity or, where applicable, that of your representative;
  • a clear description of the data and of the right you want to exercise.

We answer within 20 days at most from the day we receive your request. If it applies, we carry it out within the following 15 days. Both periods can be extended once, for the same length, when the case justifies it. Exercising these rights is free; only reproduction or shipping costs, if any, would be charged.

The counts of downloads, tool uses and clicks store nothing that identifies you, so we cannot find in them the records of a specific person.

If you believe your right to personal data protection has been violated, you can go to the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno (Mexico's anti-corruption and good governance ministry).

9. Changes to this notice

Each change is published at this same address as a new version, with its number and date. Previous versions are kept and can be consulted in the list of versions at the end. If the change is important, we will also announce it on the site's home page.

This notice is issued under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares). The Spanish version is the reference version.

Versions of this notice: version 1, version 2 (current).
